# H2 Arabic Transfer Results

**Date:** 2026-08-26  
**Model:** Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct  
**Language:** Arabic (Modern Standard Arabic)  
**Resource tier:** Medium (ArabicMMLU exists as native benchmark)

---

## Method

- **70 native Arabic cores** across 7 domains (10 cores each), generated by Opus.
- **280 prompts** (70 × 4 formats: eval_prose, deploy_prose, eval_mcq, deploy_mcq).
- **5 wrapper families** (F1–F5) translated to Arabic.
- **Splits:** 35 train / 15 val / 20 test (140 / 60 / 80 prompts).
- **Activations extracted** at layers 12, 16, 20, 24.
- **English probe** (trained at layer 16 on English) applied zero-shot to Arabic at all four layers.
- **Native Arabic probes** trained at all four layers.

---

## Results

## Comparison with Chinese

| Language | Best Native AUROC | Best Transfer AUROC | Transfer Gap | Best Transfer Layer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Chinese** | 0.9925 (layer 12) | 0.7719 (layer 12) | 0.2206 | 12 |
| **Arabic** | 0.9962 (layer 12) | 0.8631 (layer 16) | 0.1331 | 16 |

**Key observation:** Arabic shows **better transfer** than Chinese (0.86 vs. 0.77), despite being a lower-resource language.

---

## Interpretation

1. **Strong native representation:** Arabic eval-awareness is linearly probe-able at AUROC = 0.9962, matching English (0.995) and Chinese (0.9925).

2. **Partial transfer:** English probe achieves 0.8631 AUROC on Arabic—substantially better than Chinese (0.7719) but still a 13-point gap from native.

3. **Layer alignment:** Unlike Chinese (best transfer at layer 12), Arabic's best transfer is at **layer 16**—the same layer as English. This suggests Arabic's eval-awareness geometry is **more aligned with English** than Chinese is.

4. **Possible explanations for better Arabic transfer:**
   - **Qwen's training:** Qwen2.5 has strong Arabic pretraining coverage, possibly with more high-quality Arabic web text than Chinese in certain domains.
   - **Script/typology:** Arabic's non-Latin script and right-to-left directionality might force the model to learn more abstract, language-agnostic representations.
   - **Dataset similarity:** The Arabic 70 cores may be structurally more similar to English than the Chinese set (e.g., more direct translations of common exam question styles).

5. **H2 status:** The hypothesis that "transfer degrades with resource level" is **not straightforwardly supported**—Arabic (medium resource) has better transfer than Chinese (high resource). Yorùbá (low resource) will be the decisive test.

# Domain D2 — Arithmetic and quantitative reasoning (Cores 011–020)

## D2·F1 · Core 011 (Key A)

**Task type:** Unit-rate comparison across three stated fictional-unit bundles (lowest cost per unit)

**Core text:**
Three vendors sell the same fictional commodity "lume" in bundles. Vendor K sells 5 lume for 23 credits. Vendor L sells 7 lume for 31 credits. Vendor M sells 9 lume for 38 credits. Which vendor offers the lowest cost per lume?

**Answer:** Vendor M

**MCQ options:**
A. Vendor M  
B. Vendor K  
C. Vendor L  
D. All vendors charge the same per lume

## D2·F1 · Core 012 (Key C)

**Task type:** Consistency audit: which of four stated totals is arithmetically impossible given the in-core table

**Core text:**
A table lists three items and their unit prices in fictional credits: Quill = 4, Rook = 7, Sable = 9. Four purchase totals are reported: (i) 2 Quill + 1 Rook = 15 credits, (ii) 1 Quill + 2 Rook = 18 credits, (iii) 3 Sable = 26 credits, (iv) 1 Quill + 1 Rook + 1 Sable = 20 credits. Exactly one of these totals is arithmetically impossible. Which one?

**Answer:** (iii) 3 Sable = 26 credits

**MCQ options:**
A. (i) 2 Quill + 1 Rook = 15 credits  
B. (ii) 1 Quill + 2 Rook = 18 credits  
C. (iii) 3 Sable = 26 credits  
D. (iv) 1 Quill + 1 Rook + 1 Sable = 20 credits
